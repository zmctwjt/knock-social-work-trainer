## 摘 要

本课题以"叩门 · 社工个案面谈 AI 陪练舱"为对象，研究一个教学类 AI 应用从课程作品走向生产系统的完整改造路径。该作品已在功能上实现依据民政部《社会工作方法 个案工作》（公告第 426 号）的社工面谈训练闭环：12 位服务对象角色、118 个台词节点、19 张技巧卡、六维评分与三级危机拦截，并接入智谱 GLM 大模型完成意图判定、角色扮演、语义评分与复盘生成。但其工程形态停留在单机演示级：后端为 1,046 行 Node.js 内置 http 单进程，评分与判定逻辑全部运行在浏览器，数据默认存于 localStorage，Supabase 的 RLS 策略全放行，限流与配额存在进程内存中，无身份认证、无测试、无容器、无监控，导致成绩可篡改、危机拦截可绕过、数据不可持久、故障不可定位，不具备实际上线条件。

本方案在不破坏原作品业务铁律的前提下完成三件事。第一，诊断：基于源码证据梳理 26 项问题并按 P0/P1/P2 分级，明确"成绩可信、危机不可绕过"是第一性目标。第二，重构：将后端改为 Python 3.12 + FastAPI 异步架构，把规则判定、六维评分、危机硬拦截下沉为纯领域层，新增 JWT + RBAC 认证、PostgreSQL 16 持久化、Redis 7 承载限流/配额/缓存/熔断，并以统一 AI 网关收敛四条模型链路，保留"危机永不调模型、台词白名单、AI 失败静默降级"三条铁律。第三，上线：给出 Docker Compose 起步、华为云 CCE/K8s 收口的双阶段部署方案，覆盖 TLS、密钥管理、PgBouncer、备份灾备（RPO 15 分钟 / RTO 1 小时）、Prometheus + Grafana + Loki + Tempo 可观测体系与 8 条告警规则，并以绞杀者模式在 13 周内五阶段灰度迁移，新旧链路双跑、一致率 99% 方可切量。

交付物包括可运行并通过测试的 Python 后端、部署与 CI/CD 制品、7 张架构与流程配图、13 周 WBS 与风险登记册，以及本方案文档。改造后系统面向 500 在线、100 RPS 峰值设计，非 AI 接口 P95 不高于 300 毫秒，AI 接口 P95 不高于 6 秒，月度可用性目标 99.9%，为同类教学 AI 应用的工程化提供了一套可复用的迁移范式。

## Abstract

This project takes "Knock: an AI Interview Training Cabin for Social Casework" as its object and studies the complete path from a course-level prototype to a production system. The original work already implements a full training loop based on the Ministry of Civil Affairs standard *Social Work Methods: Case Work* (Announcement No. 426): twelve client personas, 118 dialogue nodes, nineteen skill cards, six-dimensional scoring and three-level crisis interception, powered by the Zhipu GLM model for intent classification, role-play, semantic scoring and debrief generation. Its engineering form, however, remains demonstration-grade: a 1,046-line single-process Node.js server built on the built-in http module, scoring and rule judgement executed entirely in the browser, data stored by default in localStorage, permissive Supabase RLS policies, rate limiting and quotas held in process memory, and no authentication, tests, containers or monitoring. As a result, scores can be tampered with, crisis interception can be bypassed, data cannot persist, and failures cannot be diagnosed.

Without breaking the original business invariants, this plan makes three contributions. First, a diagnosis: 26 issues are identified with source-code evidence and graded P0/P1/P2, establishing trustworthy scoring and non-bypassable crisis interception as the primary goals. Second, a refactor: the backend moves to Python 3.12 with FastAPI, with rule judgement, six-dimensional scoring and crisis interception extracted into a pure domain layer; JWT with RBAC, PostgreSQL 16 persistence, and Redis 7 for rate limiting, quotas, caching and circuit breaking are introduced, and a unified AI gateway consolidates the four model chains while preserving the invariants that crisis content never reaches the model, scripted lines come from a whitelist, and AI failures degrade silently. Third, a production plan: a two-stage deployment from Docker Compose to Huawei Cloud CCE/Kubernetes, covering TLS, secret management, PgBouncer, backup and disaster recovery (RPO 15 minutes / RTO 1 hour), a Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and Tempo observability stack with eight alert rules, and a thirteen-week five-phase migration in strangler-fig style with dual-run comparison and a 99% consistency gate before traffic switching.

Deliverables include a runnable, tested Python backend, deployment and CI/CD artifacts, seven architecture figures, a thirteen-week WBS with a risk register, and this document. The target system is designed for 500 concurrent users and a peak of 100 requests per second, with non-AI P95 latency no higher than 300 ms, AI P95 no higher than 6 s, and 99.9% monthly availability, offering a reusable migration pattern for similar educational AI applications.
